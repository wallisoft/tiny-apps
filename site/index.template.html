What is TinyApps?

A little shop of one-job tools from Wallisoft. No bloat, no telemetry, no sign-up — readable scripts and small installable apps you can open before you run. Several come from tinkering inside the bigger Visualised family.

✓ Verified

Submitted through GitHub and reviewed by us before it lands. One folder — a short app.vml, a 300×300 icon, and its scripts or release downloads — and a pull request. The repo's Submitting an app guide walks you through it.

⚠ Unverified

Uploaded by its author with the TinyApps upload script, no review. Quicker to publish, but it’s on you to read the script or check the download first — same as anything off the open web.
